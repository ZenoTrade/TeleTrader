ZENO COPIER - QUICK START

You need: Windows 10/11 (a PC or a Windows VPS that stays on), MetaTrader 5
installed and logged in once, and your Zeno license key (Zeno bot: /key).

1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. C:\ZenoCopier.
2. Run ZenoSetup.exe. Paste your license key, create your own report bot
   with @BotFather, press Start in it, click "Find my chat", choose your risk,
   and save.
3. ZenoAdmin opens. Click Add User and enter your MT5 login, password, server
   and the path to terminal64.exe. Click Test Connection. Your password is
   stored encrypted on this computer only. Set the account as Master.
4. Run ZenoCopier.exe and leave it open. It reports every trade to your bot.

Plans with Zeno Signals execute Zeno's signals automatically. With a
Copier-only plan, add your report bot as an admin of the channel you want to
copy and put that channel's id in SIGNAL_CHAT_ID in the .env file.

Start on a demo account first. Trading carries a high risk of loss. Zeno
provides software and general information, not financial advice.
